# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to help! Bug reports, ideas and pull requests are welcome.

## Licensing of contributions

Promodoro's code is licensed under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). To keep the project's licensing flexible (for example, to offer a paid or hosted edition later), contributions need two things:

1. **Sign off every commit** with `git commit -s`. This adds a `Signed-off-by:` line and means you agree to the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/): you wrote the contribution (or have the right to submit it).
2. **You license your contribution to the project under the AGPL-3.0, and you also grant Anzar Sharif (@anzrtone) a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use, modify and sublicense it under any license, including proprietary ones.** By opening a pull request you agree to this.

If you can't agree to #2, open an issue instead and describe the change so I can write it myself.

## Artwork

The Pomo mascot, name and icons are reserved (see [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md)). Pull requests that add or change artwork must come with a written grant of the same kind as #2 above, or I can't merge them.
